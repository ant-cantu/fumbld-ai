1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device by websites that you visit. They are widely used in order to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for a very limited and specific purpose on our website. The primary use is to enhance your user experience.

This cookie is essential for providing a seamless and convenient experience.

We do not use cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics purposes.

By checking the "Remember Me" button during login, you consent to the placement of this cookie on your device. If you do not check this box, a temporary session cookie may be used, which is deleted when you close your browser. By continuing to use our service, you agree to our use of essential cookies.

4. Managing Cookies

You can manage and/or delete cookies as you wish. You can delete all cookies that are already on your computer and you can set most browsers to prevent them from being placed. If you do this, however, you may have to manually adjust some preferences every time you visit a site and some services and functionalities (like the "Remember Me" feature) may not work.
